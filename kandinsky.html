HOW TO USE

Sing, scream, clap, snap, or just play some music.
From opera arias to sneezing—if it makes noise, it makes art!

GEOMETRY OF SOUND
Loudness = Size.
Pitch = Shape & Color.
WHAT IS C, D, E...?
These are Musical Notes. Each note triggers a specific Shape and Color (e.g. C = Blue Circle).
