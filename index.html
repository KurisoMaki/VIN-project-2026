HOW TO USE

Sing, scream, clap, snap, or just play some music.
From opera arias to sneezing—if it makes noise, it makes art!

PAINTING WITH SOUND
Loudness = Brush Size.
Pitch = Color.
WHAT IS C, D, E...?
These are Musical Notes. The AI detects the pitch of your voice. Low notes (like C) pick one color, high notes (like B) pick another.
